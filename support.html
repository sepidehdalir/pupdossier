Guide & support

A clear record.
A calmer handoff.

Start with what you already have. Keep the original close and share only what you choose.

Pupdossier is in production and is not yet available on the App Store. This guide describes version 1.0.

Start a dossier

In Pets, add a pet and optionally choose an owned photo or take one with Camera. Add a care record, select its type and enter the actual details. Keep clinical instructions as supplied by your veterinarian. The app does not calculate doses or tell you when care is due.

Keep sources and corrections

Open a pet’s Documents & source files to choose a PDF, JPEG or PNG. PDFs can be up to 20 MB and 100 pages and must be unencrypted. Original PDF bytes and metadata are retained. Images are processed locally to at most 1,600 pixels and 2 MB. Use Open or Save a copy to access a source.

A replacement keeps the same retained source slot and sends the previous file to recoverable Trash. Free includes five retained source slots per pet plus a separate profile photo. Moving a source to Trash does not release its slot. Restore and existing corrections remain available.

Dates and weights

Enter a due day, time and time zone only when you want to keep that specific date. In Settings, enable Generic local reminders and allow the iOS prompt. Up to 50 future dates can be queued. If access is off, enable it in iOS Settings and refresh the reminder queue. Notification delivery is not guaranteed.

Record weights in kg or lb with up to three decimal places. Changing the unit label does not convert the number you typed. History keeps exact entered values; the chart shows recorded observations without health ranges or predictions.

Make a handoff

Choose Create a care record PDF, select A4 or Letter and inspect included fields. Private notes, contacts and identifiers start off. Generate and preview the complete PDF, then save it in Files or use Share or print. Confirm the saved file opens before relying on it.

The basic full-record PDF is Free. Lifetime Pro adds custom Vet visit and Caregiver handoff purposes, date/category selections, chosen photo appendix, attachment references and CSV. A summary is not a vaccination certificate and does not establish fitness for boarding or travel.

Back up and restore

Settings → Save full attachment backup includes metadata, private records, retained attachments and Trash. Choose a destination you control. Keep a separate copy before deleting the app or changing devices. The total retained source budget is 11 GiB so a full backup fits below the 12 GiB backup limit; technical record/file limits also apply.

Import as archived copies adds fresh archived pets and remaps linked records and photos. It keeps current work and does not enable imported reminders. Open Archived to check the imported history, then return a pet to active when needed. Free allows one active pet; existing records and recovery remain accessible.

Common questions

Where are my records stored?

In protected local app storage. There is no developer account or cloud-sync backend. Device backups, Files providers and receiving apps have separate policies. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple account used for the purchase. Apple manages purchases. Check the local price before confirming any purchase. If a restore fails, preserve your records and send support the visible error without credentials.

What if a file will not open?

Keep the source and a full backup. Check its type, encryption, size and whether the Files provider has downloaded it. Unsupported or damaged files are rejected rather than silently replacing saved data. An unreadable library offers explicit preservation and recovery; preserve the original first.

Does it tell me which care my pet needs?

No. It records your entries and dates. Confirm care, doses, requirements and certificate validity with your veterinarian or receiving provider.
