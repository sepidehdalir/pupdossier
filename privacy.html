Privacy policy

Pupdossier keeps the pet care records you enter in protected local app storage.

Effective October 25, 2026 · Pupdossier 1.0 · Publisher: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir

The app is in production and has not yet been released. Apple’s App Privacy declaration is pending publisher approval and publication.

Local records and sources

Pet profiles, entered care records, contacts, microchip and insurance references, private notes, recorded weights, due dates, reminder history and selected attachments are processed locally. Pupdossier has no developer account, backend, advertising, tracking or analytics integration that receives these records.

Camera access is requested when you choose Camera. Apple’s photo picker supplies only the photos you select; the app does not request broad Photos read access. Chosen images are normalized locally, resized and stripped of private camera/location metadata. Technical dimensions and color information may remain. Original PDFs retain their supplied content and metadata. User-selected Save Image may request add-only Photos access.

Optional local reminders

Reminders require opt-in and system permission. Their wording does not display pet names or clinical details. Only future dates and times explicitly entered by you use the saved time zone. iOS controls delivery. The app does not send remote push notifications or generate a medical schedule.

Purchases

Apple manages Lifetime Pro purchase and restore. The app reads verified StoreKit entitlements on the device. Payment details and Apple credentials are not entered into or sent to the developer through Pupdossier. Apple’s account, purchase and App Store practices apply separately.

Exports, Files and backups

You choose whether and where to save or share. Private notes, contacts, microchip and insurance information start excluded from a PDF. You may deliberately include them. PDF and CSV contents depend on your selected purpose and fields; preview the PDF and check files before sharing. A full safety backup includes private records, retained original files and Trash. Protect any copies you save.

Files providers, receiving apps and operating-system/device backups use their own settings and policies. Pupdossier does not implement developer cloud sync or automatically upload support records.

Retention and recovery

Recoverable Trash retains records and linked files. Corrections keep previous source bytes, and Trash continues to count toward retained source limits. Imports create fresh archived pets and preserve current work. Imported reminders start off. Unreadable originals must be preserved before explicit recovery or replacement. The app has no permanent-delete control.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage. Save a full backup first if you need to retain your work. Exported copies, emails you send and device backups remain managed separately by you and the relevant providers.

Support

If you email support, we receive what you choose to send and use it to address your request. The app does not automatically attach records, photos, backups or diagnostics. Send only the information needed; do not send passwords or purchase credentials.

This website

This static website contains no advertising or analytics script. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; the hosting provider’s privacy statement applies to website requests, separately from local pet records.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@arvinify.com with privacy questions. We will update this policy and the applicable App Store responses if the app’s practices change.
